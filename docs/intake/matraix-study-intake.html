Step 4

Target population

Describe the cohort you want simulated. We will map your description to the available persona dimensions.

For example: 60% ages 18–24 and 40% ages 25–34; 50% existing customers and 50% prospects. If unknown, write “recommend.”
Describe the intended balance between cohorts and any important composition within each cohort. If unknown, write “recommend.”
Step 5

Outcomes and success

Tell us how you will decide whether the study is useful.

List the outcomes you care about and, if known, what result would be meaningful—for example, trust, signup intent, task completion, willingness to pay, or a difference between alternatives.
Provide any current human, historical, operational, or expert benchmark. If no baseline exists, leave this blank.
Step 6

Desired deliverables

Describe what you would like to receive when the study is complete.

Examples include a decision report, presentation, persona or segment dataset, raw results, recommendations, or another format.
