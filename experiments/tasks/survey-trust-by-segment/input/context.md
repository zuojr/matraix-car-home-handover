# AI recommendations brief

An app you already use has introduced AI-generated recommendations. When you
open it, a panel suggests a plan, some content, and a next action, each with a
one-line rationale like "Based on how teams like yours work."

The recommendations are helpful but generic — they do not know your budget,
constraints, or how much you trust automated advice. Answer the questionnaire
based on how you personally would react to relying on these suggestions.
